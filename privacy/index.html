BarterByte Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 20 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BarterByte ("we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our platform (the "Platform").

Section 1 - Information We Collect

Account Information: name, email address, profile photo, and location (city-level) that you provide when creating an account.

Listing and Trade Information: items you list, photos you upload, trade proposals, and messages you send to other users through the Platform.

Shipping Information: shipping address, phone number, and package details when you use the Platform's shipping-label feature.

Identity Verification: if you choose to complete identity verification, this is performed by our third-party provider, Stripe Identity. We do not collect, view, or store your government ID images or documents. We only receive and store the verification result (verified or not verified) from Stripe.

Payment Information: if you make a payment (such as for a shipping label), payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We do not collect or store your full card number or payment details — Stripe handles this directly.

Usage Information: basic information about how you use the app, such as pages visited and actions taken, to help us improve the Platform.

Section 2 - How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

Create and manage your account.

Facilitate listings, trade proposals, and messaging between users.

Calculate and display your Trust Limit.

Generate shipping labels and process related payments through Stripe.

Verify identity through Stripe Identity, when you choose to use this feature.

Communicate with you about your account and trades.

Improve and maintain the Platform.

Detect and prevent fraud or violations of our Terms of Service.

Section 3 - How We Share Information

With other users: your name, profile photo, location (city-level), listings, reviews, Trust Limit status, and verification badge (if verified) are visible to other users as part of using the Platform. Messages you send are visible to the user(s) in that conversation.

With service providers: we share information with third parties who help us operate the Platform:

Stripe — for identity verification (Stripe Identity) and payment processing (shipping label payments). Stripe's own privacy policy governs how they handle this information.

Shippo — for generating shipping labels and tracking, which requires sharing shipping addresses.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of BarterByte, our users, or others.

Section 4 - Your Choices

You can access and update most of your account information directly within the app (profile, listings, etc.). You may delete your account at any time, which will remove your profile information, though some information (such as past trade/review history relevant to other users, or records required for legal/fraud-prevention purposes) may be retained as necessary.

Section 5 - Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Section 6 - Children's Privacy

BarterByte is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Section 7 - Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
